# Brag Plan: Cariberry

## What is this app?
A soft, minimal desktop pet for Mac and Windows who lives at the bottom of your screen, keeps you focused while you study, and closes Reels tabs when you drift.

## The angle
Cozy, not corporate. Cariberry is a *bestie*, not a productivity tool. The video is the product doing its thing: pick an animal, dress her, hit "Start 25 min focus", and watch her guard your tabs. Every frame uses the app's own rendered art and panel UI.

## Hook (0–3s)
"Your desktop just got a bestie." Words slam in beside the coquette-set kitten bouncing onto the screen.

## Key moments
- Eight animals pop in two-by-two on the beat ("Pick your bestie.").
- Closet panel slides in; six outfit sets drop in one by one ("Mix & match 8 slots at once.").
- Cursor clicks "Start 25 min focus" in the real Focus panel; "She sits beside you with a book. Boba on your break."
- "Close Reels tabs for me" toggles on; vibe chips land one by one: Gentle Cozy / Sassy Bestie / Drill Sergeant.

## Outro / punchline
Wordmark "Cariberry" slams on the strong cue: "The World's Cutest Desktop Companion & Study Bestie", with macOS, Windows, 100% on-device.

## User flow worth showing
Pick her → dress her → start a focus session → she guards your tabs.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: cozy pastel launch film for a study bestie
- Interpretation: soft crossfades, bouncy pop-ins, comfortable holds; warmth over hype.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23s

## Visual identity (from the project: "coquette" theme in windows-app/src/shared/data.js)
- Background: #FFF7FA → #F6F2FE
- Accent: #CCB3F7 (lavender), second #FFBDD6; hot gradient #F57AB3 → #B88AF2
- Text: #524263 (soft #6B5E7E)
- Display/body font: SF Pro Rounded (the app's rounded UI face)
- Strongest visual: the 8 vector-drawn animals + the 4-tab panel

## Share copy (draft)
Meet Cariberry: a tiny desktop bestie who sits at the bottom of your screen, studies with you, and closes your Reels tabs. Mac + Windows, 100% on-device.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with motion-matched accents
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves vol-1 (120 BPM), trimmed to 23s with baked fade-out
- Music treatment: 0.35 volume, fade out from 20.6s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues 16.02, 17.02, 18.52, 20.02. Locks: chip 3 at 18.52, wordmark slam at 20.02. Beat grid pairs for the 8 animals (3.52 / 4.02 / 4.53 / 5.03) and outfit items (8.02 → 10.52).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass swells the background glows. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched (card sounds for pop-ins, click for simulated clicks, soft bell on the outro)
- Restraint rule: nothing sharp or repeated enough to fatigue.

## Storyboard
1. Hook — 3.02s (0–3.02): headline words slam; kitten bounces in. Crossfade →
2. Pick your bestie — 4.5s (3.02–7.52): 8 animals in pairs on beats; sub "She naps. She begs for treats." Crossfade →
3. Dress her up — 4.0s (7.52–11.52): closet panel + 6 outfit sets one by one. Crossfade →
4. Study with me — 4.0s (11.52–15.52): Home + Focus panels, cursor clicks Start. Crossfade →
5. Reels guard — 4.0s (15.52–19.52): Settings panel, toggle on, 3 vibe chips. Crossfade →
6. Outro — 3.5s (19.52–23.02): wordmark slam, tagline, platform pills.

Sequential text holds: chips ≥1.0s each settled; outfit labels are texture.
