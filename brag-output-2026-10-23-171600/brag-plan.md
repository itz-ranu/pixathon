# Brag Plan: Pixaquest

## What is this app?
Pixaquest is a free puzzle site with 14 brain games where every win earns XP and coins, ranks you up, and unlocks coupons and cash rewards, plus friend duels and a character builder.

## The angle
A premium product-launch film that shows the whole loop in 25 seconds: play a puzzle, watch the rank ring fill, turn a coupon into the reward you want, build a character, then duel friends in a room you created with a link. No fake claims: every screen is rebuilt from the real product UI, colours, copy and the real avatar builder code.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The logo tile pops in on the beat, then two lines land in the site's own words: "Train your brain." and (accent lime) "Earn real rewards."

## Key moments (the middle)
- 14 game cards slide in one by one (real names, icons, tags), then a cursor taps Memory Match.
- Memory Match plays: cards flip, pairs lock with a combo, "Puzzle solved", 3 stars, "+120 XP" counts up, the level ring fills and "Level 5 · Sharp Mind" lands.
- Rewards: milestone track, claim a 400-credit coupon, redeem it for "PayPal cash $2" from the reward list.
- Character builder: one character changes hair, stripes, face and crown in quick steps.
- Versus: host creates a room, friends join one by one, host removes a troll, presses Start, live race strip, ranked results with points.

## Outro / punchline
"Pixaquest" with the real logo, "Solve. Rank up. Earn rewards." and a "Play free" button.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: pick a puzzle. Key action: win it and level up. Result: coupon, redeem, cash reward. Plus social: create room, invite, kick, start, results.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: confident product-launch film for a game that pays you back
- Interpretation: smooth slides and crossfades on the beat, clean feature cards, a real UI in every scene, no gags, restrained polished audio.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0e1013 (cards #15181d / #1c2027)
- Accent: #d6ff3d (lime), supporting game colours #8b7bff #3dd6a5 #ffb454 #ff7aa8 #5aa9ff
- Text: #f1efe9
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque 600
- Body font: system UI
- Strongest visual element: per-game coloured art cards, the level ring, the character builder

## Share copy (draft)
Pixaquest is live: 14 puzzle games, a rank ladder that pays out in coupons and cash, and friend duels you start with a link.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, ~0.5 volume, fades out over the last 1.7s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Scene cuts land on beats at 3.0, 7.0, 11.0, 15.0, 18.0, 22.5. Strong cues 17.02, 18.52, 20.02, 23.02 used for versus start and outro.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; background glow breathes on the beat grid. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: logo pop, line reveals, card flips, level-up, claim and redeem presses, players joining, outro bell
- Restraint rule: music stays under the SFX; no sound on every card in the 14-card grid.

## Storyboard

Logo tile pops, two lines land.

Audio intent: confident, warm

### Scene 2 — Pick a puzzle — 4.0s
14 real game cards stagger in; cursor taps Memory Match.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 14 cards one by one; simulated tap
Audio intent: light, rhythmic
Audio-coupled idea: select click on tap
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

Memory Match flips and combos, solved, stars, XP count-up, level ring.
Sequential/interaction: yes — pair-by-pair flips; XP counter
Audio intent: rising reward feel
Audio-coupled idea: click per match, bell on level-up

### Scene 4 — Level up. Cash out. — 4.0s
Milestone track, claim coupon, redeem for PayPal cash $2.
Sequential/interaction: yes — claim press, redeem press, pick reward
Audio intent: satisfying confirmations
Audio-coupled idea: switch on claim, drop on redeem
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Make it yours — 3.0s
One character changes step by step; option chips highlight.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 5 quick looks

Transition mood: clean → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Duel your friends — 4.5s
Room lobby, four friends join, one removed, Start, live race, results.
Sequential/interaction: yes — joins, kick, start press
Audio intent: building energy, payoff on results

### Scene 7 — Outro — 2.5s
Logo, name, tagline, Play free.
