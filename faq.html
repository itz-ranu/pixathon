FAQ

How Pixaquest works

How do I earn rewards?

Finish puzzles to earn XP and coins. XP raises your level. At milestone levels you unlock coupons, coins, or cash rewards. Daily bonuses, quests and streaks give extra coins.

What is a coupon and how do I use it?

A coupon has a credit value. Open the Redeem page, press Redeem on a coupon, and choose any reward it covers, such as brand deals, mobile top-up, gift cards or cash. Leftover credit turns into coins.

How do coins become coupons?

On the Redeem page, pick a coupon pack and pay with coins. Bigger packs give bonus credit.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded ads are optional and double the rewards for one round. Normal ads appear at the sides of the page and occasionally between rounds.

When do I get cash or gift cards?

Those rewards are reviewed first, usually within 7 days. We may ask you to verify your identity. See the Terms.

Why was my reward declined?

Usually because we found cheating, multiple accounts, or missing or wrong payout details.

Is my progress saved?

Right now it is saved in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases it. Accounts that sync across devices are planned.

What does the streak do?

Playing on consecutive days builds a streak that grows your daily bonus and gives a small XP boost.

What is the promo code box?

Enter a code we share on our channels to get free coins or a coupon. Each code works once.
