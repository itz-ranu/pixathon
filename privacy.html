How we use it

  • To run the site, calculate rewards and prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To deliver rewards you request.
  • To show advertising and measure how it performs.

We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and ads

  • Essential storage: remembers your progress and settings. Required for the site to work.
  • Advertising cookies: our ad partner (for example Google AdSense) may use cookies to show and measure ads. If you choose “Keep non-personalised”, we ask the partner to show non-personalised ads only. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

You can also clear storage or block cookies in your browser; the game may then forget your progress.

Sharing

We share data only with: advertising partners (as above), payout providers (to send your reward), and authorities when the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may ask to access, correct, delete, or export your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details on the FAQ page. Clearing your browser storage deletes progress stored on your device.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Rewards that need payout details are for adults only.

Retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable technical measures, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
